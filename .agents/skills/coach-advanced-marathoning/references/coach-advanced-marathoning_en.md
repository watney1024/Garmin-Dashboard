# Advanced Marathoning `advanced_marathoning`

> ⚠ **Provenance & copyright**: this file is this repo's **summary + application guide**
> of the ideas in Pete Pfitzinger & Scott Douglas, *Advanced Marathoning*, not a
> copy/excerpt of the book.

## 1. Origin & fit

- Source: Pete Pfitzinger & Scott Douglas, *Advanced Marathoning*
- Core idea: treat the marathon like a project — **a long ~18-week cycle, two quality
  sessions and one 20 km+ long run per week**; the long run regularly mixes in
  **marathon-pace (MP) segments**; several weekly-volume levels (≈55/70/85-mile plans)
  suit different runners.
- **Good for**: marathoners with 1–2 years of systematic training who can invest 6–8 h/week
  and tolerate higher volume.
- **Not for**: first-timers or runners under ~40 km/week (start with a gentler package);
  those who cannot manage 5–6 runs/week.

## 2. Intensity system & pace source

| type | code | basis | typical session |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recovery | Rec | very slow, HR/feel | `Rec 8–10 km` after quality |
| General aerobic | GA | upper E | `GA 12–16 km` |
| Long run | LR | E start, incl. **MP segments** | `LR 20–32 km (incl. M 8–14 km)` |
| Lactate threshold | LT | ≈15K–half pace | `LT 6–13 km` or `2×20 min` |
| VO2max intervals | VO2 | ≈3–5K pace | `5×600–1000` (rest ≈ rep time) |

- Pace source: GA/E by feel + HR; LT/VO2/MP from the VDOT table zones (docs/09). The book
  carries its own tables; this repo uses its approximation tool and notes the difference.

## 3. Weekly skeleton construction

1. Typical pattern: **Sunday long run (with MP segments)**, **Tuesday quality (LT or VO2,
   alternating)**, GA/medium-long on Sat/Wed, recovery elsewhere — mapped onto the
   profile's available days. This package wants `quality_days ≥2` and `runs_per_week ≥5`.
2. Peak long runs 28–34 km (longer for higher VDOT); **MP segments go into some long runs,
   not every one**.
3. ≥48 h between LT and the long run; VO2 sessions never land the day before a long run.
4. Volume +10–15%/week, obeying the generic safety layer; a recovery week (every 3–4 weeks)
   cuts volume but keeps sessions.
5. Weekly volume follows the profile's available time — do not force a higher "mileage
   level" than the runner can sustain.

## 4. Periodization & volume ramp

- Reference structure: **base 6–8 weeks** (GA-focussed, long runs ramp up) → **specific
  8–10 weeks** (LT/VO2 alternating, MP long runs lengthen) → **taper 2–3 weeks** (volume
  down 20–40%; keep LT and a little MP; last quality session 10–14 days before the race).
- Total quality load: this school stresses **full recovery between quality sessions**;
  consecutive hard days are discouraged.

## 5. Tests & gates

- Use a non-A half as a combined "long run + MP" test (drop the week before to 60–70%).
- This package's gate: does the peak LR's MP segment hit target (e.g. 26 km incl. M12 with
  a stable M pace)? If yes the goal stands; otherwise race-day starts at the conservative
  band (consistent with docs/06 — one-vote veto).

## 6. Red lines & cautions

- **Recovery after quality is mandatory**: Rec is part of the structure, not optional.
- MP segments "simulate the race", not "chase pace" — in bad weather/form they downgrade to
  "effort-based MP".
- High mileage is a feature of this school; yellow/red criteria are NOT relaxed because of
  it (docs/03/06).

## 7. Strength / auxiliary advice

- Strength 2×/week; put lower-body heavy work on Wednesday (≥72 h before the Sunday LR);
  calf/glute-medius/core are the floor.

## 8. Worked example

5–6 runs/week: `Tue LT 10 km` · `Wed Rec 8` · `Thu GA 12` · `Sat Rec/GA 10` ·
`Sun LR 24 (incl. MP 10)`.
